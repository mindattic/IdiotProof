## Method

- **Collection:** `tools/momentum/fetch_momentum.py` keeps shorts whose title starts with TOP/Top or carries a date and saves YouTube captions per trade day; `momentum import` upserts them (idempotent).
- **Extraction:** the transcript goes to the LLM in the user message only (untrusted text), with a strict JSON schema; levels are copied as spoken. The spoken-price resolver scales a bare number by 1, 1/10 or 1/100 to the one closest to the stock's split-consistent prior close (and the day's open): "270" = $2.70, "56" = $5.60; "$3.50", "five bucks" are literal.
- **Tickers:** each spoken ticker (spelled letters joined: "P A R A" = PARA) is validated against active Alpaca assets and the prior/same session's top movers, with one-letter fixes for misheard captions; a pick named only by description ("the one that closed up over 80%") is identified from the prior session's top gainers by the stated move and the level scale, at lower confidence.
- **Trigger:** break = a 1-minute high over the level (entry at the level, or the open when the bar opened over it); reclaim = trades under the level, then a close back over it; hold = pulls to within 3% of the level, then closes over that pullback bar's high. Triggers count from 04:00 to 15:55 ET.
- **Confirm and entry:** when he wants a pullback confirmation, the plan enters at the close of the first bar after the trigger that dips to within 2% of a confirm level (or VWAP from 04:00) and closes at or over it; otherwise at the trigger price. No confirm, no entry.
- **Targets and stop:** a target is hit when a bar's high reaches it. The invalidation is the level he states, else the lowest confirm level, else VWAP, else the trigger level, hit by a 1-minute close under it. Plan P&L exits at the first target, the stop, or the 15:55 bar's close; no costs or slippage.
- **Top gapper:** rank by the day's high over the split-consistent prior close among every active Alpaca stock with prior close >= $0.10 and >= $1M traded; top 20 counts as a real top gapper.
- **Brags:** daily split-adjusted bars over the stated window (previous session, last week, this week, or the last two weeks).

## Caveats

- Captions mishear tickers and prices; low-confidence tickers are marked and morning updates can name a different stock than the evening list.
- He gives levels from a chart we do not see; a level we cannot price ("the recent high") makes that scenario unverifiable rather than guessed.
- Entries on a gap-through or premarket trigger use prices a retail order might not get; the plan P&L is an upper bound of what following the words literally would return.
