## What this says

Written by hand after the run. The conclusions rest on the **frozen** version. The non-frozen split sensitivity
(IP-T633) changes no ranking and moves no headline number by more than a few tenths of a point.

- **Almost no gapper day comes out of nowhere at the daily-bar level, but that headline flatters the tags.**
  Under the frozen definitions only 6.8% of G20 days, 2.8% of G50 days and 2.2% of G100 days carry none of
  the tags. 37% of all eligible days carry none too. The remainder is small because four tags describe the
  general state of a small cap rather than an event: lowPriceLowAdv (18% of days), dilutionFiling90 (19%),
  eightK5 (10%) and hotTape (35%). Counting only the narrow tags (post hoc), **24% of G20 days, 11% of G50 days
  and 10% of G100 days have no nameable precondition at the D-1 close**, against 79% of all days. The bigger the
  move, the more likely something was visible the evening before. Amendment 14's follow-up rule fires only above
  30% of G50 days without a tag. Neither reading reaches that, so no data ticket is triggered. The old "70% have
  no named cause" (amendment 08) was about stored news. It was never about price or volume history.
- **The cause that dominates is "it already ran".** ranYesterday (lift 14.7), ranLast5 (7.9) and bigRunner60
  (5.4) are the first three steps of the exclusive hierarchy. Together they hold 56% of G20 days and about 71-73%
  of G50 and G100 days. Next come a crash in the last five sessions (11.5x), a D-1 volume surge (7.5x), a strong
  D-1 close (7.2x), an SSR day (5.9x) and a recent reverse split (5.5x). These are the conditions of a volatile,
  recently active name. Most of them are versions of each other.
- **Recipes raise the odds a lot, but each covers few days.** The best stable combinations reach P(G20) of 22-25%
  and P(G50) of 6-9%, against base rates of 0.96% and 0.15%. That is lift 22-26 on G20 and up to 61 on G50. Each
  one covers only 1-4% of all G20 days. Best by G50: ranLast5 + volumeSurge + subDollar (776 days, P(G50) 9.3%,
  P(G100) 3.7%). Next: ranLast5 + bigRunner60 + volumeSurge (944 days, 8.4%) and bigRunner60 + volumeSurge +
  subDollar (864 days, 8.2%). "Stable" does not filter much: 407 of the 409 combinations with at least 500 days
  pass. Almost every tag has lift > 2 alone, and a combination of two of them nearly always does too. The useful
  ordering is the lift and the absolute rate, not the flag.
- **G20 is a high-water mark, not a trade.** A name up 20% yesterday usually swings 20% intraday today. P(G20)
  of 22% says nothing about whether a long at any price makes money. Earlier work found that buying gappers
  already up 20% at the open loses money by the close. Nothing here is a candidate until a later amendment tests
  it with entries, exits and costs.
- **Some tags are mostly price-level selection.** On the frozen events against their matched controls (matched on
  ADV decile and price tier), subDollar, lowPriceLowAdv and hotTape have ratio 1.0. Their universe-wide lift comes
  from the price tier and liquidity they select, not from anything specific to a gapper. The tags that still
  separate events from like-for-like controls: ranYesterday (9-11x), volumeSurge (9-11x), closedStrong (5-7x),
  crashedLast5 (6x), ranLast5 (3.4x), ssrD1 (3x), reverseSplit90 (3-4x), tinyShares (2-2.6x), newListing180
  (2.6x), bigRunner60 (2-2.6x) and dilution filings (1.5-1.9x).
- **News and insider buying add little.** On candidate plus sample days (amendment 01 weights), a non-coverage
  item between the D-1 close and 04:00 on D (freshNews) has lift 6.6, but it is on only 1.4% of days and 9% of
  G20 days. 64% of G20 days have no stored non-coverage item from D-5 to 04:00 on D. A Form 4 open-market
  purchase in the prior 30 days has no lift (1.0, q 0.91), and on the frozen events it is not more common than
  on controls.

## Deviations from the frozen text and data notes

- **insiderBuy30 is only in the stratified table.** Form 4 transaction detail (`InsiderTransactions` joined to
  `ResearchClaims.Ticker`, all 40,084 P rows joined) was fetched only around candidate and sample days. A
  universe-wide tag would lean toward days that ran. So in the universe tables and combinations it counts as
  unknown, and its own row is computed on candidate plus sample days with amendment 01 weights. A purchase also
  counts only once its Form 4 was filed (ResearchClaims.ArticleDate on or before D-1), so it is knowable at the
  D-1 close. The frozen text names only the transaction date.
- **Choices the frozen text leaves open:**
  - D is the symbol's next daily bar within 5 sessions of S = D-1, as in amendment 13. Lags (D-2, D-5, D-60) count
    the symbol's own bars.
  - volumeSurge compares D-1 volume with the average of the 20 sessions before D-1, adjusted for splits. This is
    the candidate rule's convention.
  - lowPriceLowAdv uses the eligibility ADV20$ (20 sessions through D-1).
  - newListing180 needs a first bar after the data start (2021-09-01), so names listed before then are not "new".
  - bigRunner60 has a shorter window in the first months of train, because daily bars start 2021-09-01.
- **hotTape:** a date is hot when its count is strictly above the expanding 75th percentile of train dates up to
  and including it. Counts trend up over train, so 263 of 755 dates (35%) qualify, not 25%. The first run used >=
  and gave 39%. It was rerun once with the strict reading before any result was interpreted.
- **News:** `CatalystEvents` only (Alpaca news; it holds no filings). Non-coverage means CatalystType other than
  NoCatalyst. staleNews5 runs from 00:00 ET on session D-5 to 16:00 on D-1.
- **Filings:** 49% of the dilution and 8-K rows have no acceptance time. They are placed at 17:30 ET on the
  filed date (amendment 08's rule). 4,404 symbols have a CIK, and the CIK-dependent tags are known on 93% of
  eligible days.
- **tinyShares:** XBRL shares are as filed. After a reverse split the latest filed count can still be the
  pre-split number, so the tag undercounts tiny floats right after splits.
- **Zero-volume daily rows** are treated as no bar, as the coordinator asked.
- **Split sensitivity (NON-FROZEN, IP-T633):** it drops D days with open or high more than +200% over the
  adjusted D-1 close, share volume no more than the prior 20-session average, and a median close over D..D+4 at
  least 2x the D-1 close. It also drops bad prints: open or high more than +200% while the low and close stay
  under 1.5x. Such bars are neutralised in the tag history. It removes 70 days, all of them G100. Kept although
  suspicious: MTVA 2022-09-13 (D volume 2x average, so not flagged), OLOX 2024-05-02 and SVRN 2022-12-22.
  Reverse splits under 1-for-3 cannot be caught by a +200% rule.
