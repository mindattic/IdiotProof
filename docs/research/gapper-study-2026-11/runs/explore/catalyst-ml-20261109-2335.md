# Explore: machine-learning catalyst discovery (IP-T488): which news and filings move small caps

Train only. Weighted (amendment 01 population). Folds:
- fold 1 (fit 2021-11..2022-10, eval 2022-11..2023-04)
- fold 2 (fit 2021-11..2023-04, eval 2023-05..2023-10)
- fold 3 (fit 2021-11..2023-10, eval 2023-11..2024-04)
- fold 4 (fit 2021-11..2024-04, eval 2024-05..2024-10)

## 0. Data and method

Event table (`explore catalyst-events`): one row per (symbol, item) for every stored news item (Alpaca/Benzinga, headline + stored snippet, 205,726 rows) and EDGAR filing (forms 3/4/5 left out, 45,815 rows) whose event session E is a train universe day with minute bars: 251,541 rows, 134,310 symbol-days, 3,775 symbols, 2021-11-01..2024-10-31. E = the publish day for an item out 00:00-15:59 ET on a trading day, else the next session. Outcomes start at the first full minute after publication (04:00 of E for an item from the night before) against the reference = the last trade that ended by publication (the prior session's after-hours count; else the prior daily close): max run in the next 60 / 240 minutes and to 16:00 (high over reference), return to the 16:00 close, D+1 / D+2 close (daily, raw; blank across a split or past train), giveback = close over the post-publication high minus 1, and whether the day ran >= +50% / +100% (04:00-16:00 high over the adjusted prior close). Pre-publication drift: log return over the 30 / 60 minutes before publication, and as a z-score against the stock's own 20-session daily volatility scaled to the window (sigma x sqrt(minutes / 390)). Controls: log prior close, log 20-day dollar volume, ADV decile, ADR, log shares outstanding known before E (float proxy), D-1 top-mover rank, days since and log ratio of the last reverse split, daily volatility, gap at publication, the pre-drift, premarket volume before publication, publish minute and session, symbols on the item, items earlier that symbol-day, filing flag, weekday. Base rates: run >= +50% within 240 minutes 0.9 %; day >= +50% 4.7 %. The news store holds the candidate windows (gapper days and the 5 sessions before, plus controls), so the population is small caps around a move, not every small cap.

Topics (tools/gapper-catalyst-ml/catalyst_ml.py, Python sklearn TfidfVectorizer(1-2 grams, min_df 5, max_df 0.3, sublinear) + LogisticRegression(liblinear, C=1) / Ridge(alpha=10), scikit-learn, run isolated with `python -I`): TF-IDF on word 1-2-grams of headline + snippet (numbers, dollar amounts and percentages folded into tokens, exchange tickers and cashtags stripped), NMF on the 117,891 unique news items: 30 topics; the one topic holding over 15% of the items (the catch-all of press releases once the wire templates had their own topics) refit into 25 sub-topics, and a sub-topic over 4% into 15 more. A row's topic is its largest NMF weight. The 71 topics were then named by hand from their top terms and examples (tools/gapper-catalyst-ml/names.json), several templated wires merging into one name, leaving 37 news clusters. Filings are clustered by form and 8-K item (priority 1.01 > 2.01 > 1.02 > 2.02 > 3.01 > 3.02 > 3.03 > 5.03 > 5.07 > 5.02 > 7.01 > 8.01). No 6-K is in the study's EDGAR index (the widened dilution files were never pulled on this host), so there is no 6-K exhibit cluster. No external model or API.

## 1. Clusters: which catalyst types precede a run

Every row counts once (a chatty symbol-day counts once per item). Lift = P(run >= +50% within 240 minutes of publication | cluster) / 0.9 %. Run = median max run to 16:00; giveback = median close over the post-publication high minus 1; mean returns winsorized at the pooled 1st/99th percentiles. Drift share = mean pre-60-minute log drift / (that + mean log max run in the 240 minutes after), on items published 04:30-15:59 ET with premarket or regular-hours prints in the hour before ("in-session"). Kind: news = an event at the company; coverage = a wire about the move, the market or analysts' views of it (Benzinga templates); filing = EDGAR form / 8-K item. Sorted by lift.
