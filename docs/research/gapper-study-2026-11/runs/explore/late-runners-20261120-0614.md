## D2. Catalyst timing for late runners

Late runners of the D1 census. Fresh catalyst = a stored news item or EDGAR filing (8-K, 8-K/A, 6-K, S-1, S-3, 424B3/4/5, SC 13D; no acceptance time = 23:59:59 ET of the filing date) after D-1 16:00 whose amendment 08 type is not "no catalyst". Signed minutes = the time point minus the latest fresh item at or before it; when there is none before it, the first fresh item after it (by 20:00), so negative = the move came first. ## Hypotheses (train)
