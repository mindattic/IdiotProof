No entry at or after 15:45. Costs: the frozen round trip at $1,000 (Corwin-Schultz over D-1..D-5 x the premarket multiplier 9.393 from spread-multiplier.json, square-root impact on the volume from 04:00 to entry, 20-session sigma from split-adjusted daily closes, floored at 0.5 %) and with the spread component at 0.5x / 0.25x. Break-even cost = the gross mean. Capacity: `ParticipationCap` (5% of the 5 minutes before entry and of the profile-forecast shares around the actual exit), skipped under $200.
