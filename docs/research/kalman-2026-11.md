# Kalman state-space features on low-priced stocks (IP-T405)

**Verdict: no Kalman feature is promoted to a DSL condition.** The one strong effect found is bid-ask bounce, not a tradeable edge.

## Protocol (fixed before the run)

- Symbols SNDL, PLUG, LCID, TLRY, OPEN, GRAB; leader IWM for the pair residual.
- 2026-09-01..2026-10-30 regular-session 1-minute bars, from a separate research LocalDB (`IdiotProof_Research_T405`), read-only market data, no orders.
- First 67% of sessions: fit the local-linear-trend noise variances by maximum likelihood and learn quintile bucket edges. Last 33%: held-out test.
- Features: trend slope, innovation z (close vs the filter's prediction), pair-residual z against IWM (time-varying hedge ratio).
- Outcome: forward 5-minute return > 0.
- Test: two-sided exact binomial per bucket vs the held-out base rate, Bonferroni across 5 buckets.
- Promotion rule: a feature becomes a DSL condition only if its corrected p < 0.05 / 18 (6 symbols × 3 features) in at least half the symbols.

## Run 1: entry at the signal bar's close

Innovation z and pair-residual z cleared the bar in all 6 symbols. The lowest-innovation quintile rose 47–57% of the time vs a 31–43% base rate (lift 1.14–1.82, corrected p < 0.001). That is short-horizon mean reversion.

## Bounce check: entry at the next bar's open

On $1–5 stocks a $0.01 tick is 0.3–0.8% of price. A close printed at the bid "reverts" when the next print is at the ask, and nobody can buy at the bid after seeing the close.

| symbol | bucket | fwd 5m, close entry | fwd 5m, next-open entry | up%, next-open | base up% | 1 tick / price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SNDL | lowest z | +0.229% | +0.048% | 34.7% | 31.3% | 0.80% |
| SNDL | highest z | −0.222% | −0.034% | 30.5% | 31.3% | 0.80% |
| PLUG | lowest z | +0.159% | −0.023% | 36.9% | 35.9% | 0.71% |
| LCID | lowest z | +0.044% | +0.005% | 42.5% | 43.2% | 0.30% |
| TLRY | lowest z | +0.053% | −0.014% | 38.2% | 37.5% | 0.34% |
| OPEN | lowest z | +0.073% | −0.029% | 40.0% | 39.2% | 0.50% |
| GRAB | lowest z | +0.075% | −0.006% | 38.1% | 36.4% | 0.35% |

Entered at the first price a signal could actually trade at, the effect is gone: up-rates return to the base rate, and the mean move is a small fraction of one tick.

## Run 2: next-open entry (now the operation's default)

Only LCID's top slope quintile clears the pre-registered threshold (47.0% vs 42.3%, corrected p = 0.001). That's 1 of 6 symbols, and the slope effect isn't directionally consistent: SNDL's strongest bucket was its lowest slope. Nothing meets the promotion rule.

## What shipped

- `IdiotProof.Evidence/StateSpace/`: local level, local linear trend and regression (hedge-ratio) Kalman filters, MLE fitting, and causal per-bar features.
- `IdiotProof.Evidence/BucketSignificance.cs`: exact two-sided binomial per bucket with Bonferroni.
- `idiotproof evidence kalman`: entry at the next bar's open by default (`--entry close` reproduces Run 1), so bid-ask bounce can't pass for a signal again.

Up = strictly positive return, so flat bars count as not-up; that's why base rates sit below 50% on penny names.
